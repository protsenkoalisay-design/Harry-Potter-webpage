    THE SEVEN STORIES

    The Hogwarts Saga

    Original fan-made summaries of the seven main books.

    BOOK I

    Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone

    Harry discovers that magic is real and begins his first year at Hogwarts. Alongside Ron and Hermione, he learns about the castle, wizarding history and the mystery surrounding a powerful magical object.

    BOOK II

    Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets

    A hidden threat awakens within Hogwarts. Strange attacks, an ancient chamber and a mysterious diary lead Harry toward a secret connected with the school's past.

    BOOK III

    Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban

    An escaped prisoner is believed to be searching for Harry. Dementors arrive at Hogwarts while Harry uncovers secrets involving his parents and their old friends.

    BOOK IV

    Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire

    Hogwarts hosts the Triwizard Tournament. Harry unexpectedly becomes a champion and is pulled into events that lead the wizarding world toward a dangerous new chapter.

    BOOK V

    Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix

    The Ministry refuses to accept the growing danger. A new authority takes control of Hogwarts, while Harry and his friends secretly prepare students to defend themselves.

    BOOK VI

    Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince

    Harry and Dumbledore investigate memories from Voldemort's past. At the same time, Draco receives a dangerous mission and tensions inside the castle rise.

    BOOK VII

    Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows

    Harry, Ron and Hermione leave Hogwarts to search for the remaining Horcruxes. Their journey eventually brings them back to Hogwarts for the final confrontation.
